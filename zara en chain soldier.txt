Mato is a mysterious alternate world filled with dangerous monsters called Shuuki. Strange supernatural fruits known as Peaches grow throughout Mato.
When a person consumes a Peach, they can gain a unique supernatural ability. However, these powers are generally awakened in women, which is why the Demon Defense Force is primarily made up of female soldiers.
Because the Shuuki constantly threaten people traveling through Mato, the Demon Defense Force was created to protect humanity and eliminate them. The strongest Peach users become members and commanders of specialized squads.
Kyouka Uzen leads the 7th Squad, whose members include Himari Azuma, Shushu Suruga, and Nei Ookawamura.
